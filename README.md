# SafetyCopilot

**RAG-Based Industrial HSE Knowledge Assistant**

SafetyCopilot is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system built for industrial Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) teams. It ingests safety-critical documents — including HSE manuals, Safety SOPs, Risk Assessments, Lockout/Tagout procedures, Working at Height procedures, Confined Space procedures, Electrical Safety procedures, Emergency Response plans, and Incident/Near-Miss reports — and enables teams to retrieve accurate, source-cited answers grounded entirely in their own documentation.
